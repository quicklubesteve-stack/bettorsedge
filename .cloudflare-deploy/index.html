Sports Betting Explained Simply

Zero confusing jargon. How odds work, how to read lines, and the exact reasons WHY smart bets work.

🧭 The 4-Step Decision Checklist Before Any Bet
1. Who is Starting?

Starting goalies in hockey; starting QB/offensive tackles in football.

Why: A backup goalie or tackle changes the whole game!

2. Rest & Travel

Did the team play yesterday or fly across 3 time zones overnight?

Why: Tired legs collapse in the 4th quarter and commit lazy penalties.

3. Matchup Mismatch

Does a top run attack play the 31st-ranked run defense?

Why: Stylistic mismatches beat overall records every time.

4. Stick to 1-2 Units

Never risk more than 1% to 2% of your budget on any single game.

Why: Chasing losses with double-down bets is how people go broke.

Concept #1 The Basics
Plus (+) & Minus (-) Odds
Minus = Favorite • Plus = Underdog

-150: You must risk $150 to win $100 profit.
+150: You risk $100 to win $150 profit.

💡 Why: If all teams paid the same, everyone would just bet the best team. The odds balance risk so you get rewarded more for taking an underdog!

Concept #2 Outright
The Moneyline (ML)
"Who Just Wins The Game"

You don't care about points or goals. If your team wins by 1 or 40, your bet cashes.

💡 Why to Bet It: Great for close games where you believe a team will win outright, or taking value underdogs (like Vegas Golden Knights +105).

Concept #3 Head Starts
Point Spread & Puck Line
Giving or Getting Points

Favorite (-2.5): Starts with -2.5. Must win by 3+ points.
Underdog (+2.5): Starts with +2.5. Can win OR lose by 1 or 2.

💡 Why to Bet It: Turns blowouts into fair 50/50 contests so betting heavy favorites gives close to even-money return (+100 or -110).

Concept #4 Highest Hit Rate
Player Props (90%+ Conviction)
"Bet on the Player, Not the Team"

Wager on one player's stats (e.g. Mitch Marner Over 0.5 Assists, Zach Hyman Over 3.5 SOG, Josh Allen Rush TD).

💡 Why It's the Safest Bet: A team might lose by 20, but the coach never stops feeding his star player. Star volume is the most predictable variable in sports!
